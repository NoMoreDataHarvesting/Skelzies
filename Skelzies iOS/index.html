Summary

Skelzies does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Skelzies does not require an account, does not connect to any servers, and does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Player names entered during game setup are stored only in your device's memory for the duration of a game and are discarded when the app is closed. Nothing you type into Skelzies leaves the iPad it was typed on.

What we do not do

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not use cookies, pixels, fingerprints, or any tracking technology.
  • We do not include third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not include third-party analytics (no Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or similar).
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • We do not integrate Game Center, iCloud, or social sharing.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties — because we have no data to share.

Children's privacy

Skelzies is safe for all ages and rated 4+ on the App Store. Because we do not collect any information whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and Skelzies is compliant with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design.

Parents and guardians can rest assured that letting a child play Skelzies does not expose them to advertising, data collection, or contact with strangers.

Third-party services

None. Skelzies is a fully offline application. It makes no network requests. If your iPad is in airplane mode, Skelzies plays exactly the same as when it is online.

Permissions Skelzies requests

Skelzies does not request any special permissions. There is no permission prompt for the camera, microphone, contacts, location, notifications, or tracking — because Skelzies does not use any of these.

Data security

Since Skelzies does not collect or transmit data, there is no data at risk to secure against breach. The app itself is distributed through the Apple App Store and is subject to Apple's platform security protections on your device.

Your rights

Under privacy laws like the EU's GDPR and California's CCPA, you have rights to access, correct, and delete personal data held about you. Because Skelzies holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you would like written confirmation of that for your records, contact us at the email below and we'll send it.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that collect information — for example, online multiplayer, cloud saves, or optional analytics — we will:

  • Update this policy with the effective date of the change.
  • Notify users through an in-app update or the App Store release notes.
  • Where required by law, ask for your consent before collecting anything.

Older versions of this policy will remain accessible on request.
